# About

I'm Abdullah Ajmal, a data scientist with a full BS in Mathematics (University of Wah, CGPA 3.48/4.0), now pursuing an MS in Data Science at PAF-IAST, Pakistan. My mathematics background isn't a line on a résumé — it's the toolkit I work from: Bayesian inference, convex optimization, state-space modeling, and statistical learning theory.

I build end-to-end machine learning projects the way they'd be built in production: real datasets, rigorous evaluation, calibrated uncertainty, explainability, and deployment artifacts — with the failed experiments and limitations documented, not hidden.

I'm working toward roles in Data Science, Machine Learning, and AI research, and I'm especially interested in problems where statistical rigor matters: experimentation, uncertainty quantification, and optimization.
